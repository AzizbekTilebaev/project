# Ingliz tili — ne úyrenemiz?

> Maqset: **sóz + fraza + gáp** aytıw / jazıw. Kitaptaǵı «Unit 3» atı emes — *ne aytasız?*

## Basqısh boyınsha

| Basqısh | Ne úyrenesiz | Qayda |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| 1–4 | Sálem, shańaraq, reń, waqıt, bazar | **1–4** tab |
| 5–6 | Artikllar, kóplik, Present/Past Simple | **5–6** + **5/6 gram.** |
| 7–9 | Pikir, densawlıq, media, jumıs dialogı | **7–9** tab |
| 10–11 | Passive, plans, conditionals, jazıw | **10–11** + **10 Guide** |

## Hár kún (5–10 minut)

1. **3–5 jańa sóz** — dawıs shıǵarıp aytıń.  
2. **2 fraza** — óz atıńız / úyińiz menen almastırıń.  
3. **3 gáp** jazıń yamasa awızeki aytıń.

## Eń tiykarǵı qurılıslar

| Qurılıs | Misal |
|---------|--------|
| *to be* | *I am a pupil. She is seven.* |
| *have / has* | *I have a book. He has a bike.* |
| Present Simple | *I live in Nukus. She likes tea.* |
| Past Simple | *We went to school yesterday.* |
| Articles | *a pen / an apple / the sun* |
| Question | *What’s this? Where do you live?* |
| Opinion | *I think … because …* |
| Passive (10+) | *English is spoken here.* |

## Eski vs jańa

| Paydasız | Paydalı |
|----------|---------|
| Unit atları dizimi | Fraza + mashq |
| Tek «oqıń» | Óz gábińizdi jazıw / aytıw |

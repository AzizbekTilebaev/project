# 1–4 klass — ne aytamız?

> Awızeki tiykar. Kishi sózlik + qaytalaw. Grammatika — keyinirek (5–6).

## Sálemlesiw hám ózin tanıstırıw

| Inglis | QQ / mánisi |
|--------|-------------|
| Hello! / Hi! | Sálem |
| What’s your name? | Atıńız kim? |
| My name’s … / I’m … | Meniń atım … |
| How are you? — I’m fine. | Qalaysız? — Jaqsı. |
| Where are you from? — I’m from Nukus. | Qaydan? |
| How old are you? — I’m seven. | Neshe jas? |

**Mashq:** óz atıńız, jasıńız, qalanız menen 4 gáp aytıń.

## Shańaraq

**Sózler:** mother, father, sister, brother, granny, grandad, uncle, aunt, cousin  

| Fraza |
|-------|
| *This is my mother.* |
| *I’ve got two sisters.* |
| *He is my uncle.* |

**Mashq:** shańaraq súwretine qarap 5 gáp.

## Reń, oyınshıq, zat

**Reń:** red, blue, green, yellow, black, white, pink, orange  
**Zat:** pen, book, ball, bag, desk, chair  

| Fraza |
|-------|
| *What’s this? — It’s a pen.* |
| *It’s a red ball.* |
| *I have a blue book.* |

**Mashq:** úyińizdegi 5 zat + reń (*a green cup*).

## Mektep hám waqıt (2–4)

**Sózler:** school, teacher, pupil, classroom, Monday…Sunday, o’clock  

| Fraza |
|-------|
| *What day is it today?* |
| *I get up at seven o’clock.* |
| *I go to school.* |
| *My favourite subject is English.* |

**Mashq:** «Meniń kúnim» — 5 gáp (*I get up… I have breakfast…*).

## Úy hám jaylasıw (3)

**Sózler:** house, room, kitchen, bedroom, garden, window, door  

| Fraza |
|-------|
| *Our house is big.* |
| *Where is the book? — It’s on the table.* |
| *My room is small.* |

## Bazar, awqat, densawlıq (4)

| Fraza |
|-------|
| *How much is it? / How much are they?* |
| *I want bananas and apples.* |
| *I have a headache / a toothache.* |
| *I help my mum.* |

**Mashq:** bazar dialogı — 4 jup soraw–juwap.

## Kúnlik 5 minut

1. 5 sóz dawıs shıǵarıp.  
2. *I am… / I have… / I like…* — hárbirinen 1 gáp.  
3. Bir dialog: *Hello! — Hi! How are you?*
